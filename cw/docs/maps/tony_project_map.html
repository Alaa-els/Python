Effort by increment (hours)

Basis: a build stage is 1.5 to 3.5 agent hours and 1 to 2.5 human hours; a docs-only stage about half. Plus or minus 35 percent until the ledger has two increments in it. Token estimate: assumptions per stage session

The three input categories are disjoint: every input token is read from cache, written to cache, or sent uncached. Sessions = 31 stages times a retry factor (1.0 / 1.3 / 1.8); the retry factor and the cache behaviour are uncalibrated until the ledger holds two increments. Context window occupancy is not billed; account-wide percentages are not this project's consumption.
