# CCW - Gamma Test-Partner Terms v0 (draft to send Tony after the call)

- Prepared: 05-Sep-2026 (Chat C01)
- Author: Alaa Elsayed
- Purpose: one page that fixes what each side gives and gets. No build chat with Gamma data runs before it is agreed in writing (email is enough).

## 1. What this is

- Alaa Elsayed and Mo Ashour (the Builders) are building a commercial workbench for consultants that receives, checks and assesses contractor submissions.
- Gamma prepares exactly those submissions, so Gamma is the ideal test partner: its applications, variations, contracts and quotes are run through the workbench, and Gamma sees what a client's QS would see.

## 2. Gamma gives

- Real test data with permission: two applications for payment, two variation proposals with build-ups, two subcontracts, one set of supplier quotes with the client's schedule. Redacted where Gamma wishes.
- Tony's time: one half-hour call at the start of each of the five phases, and feedback within a week of each mirror run.
- Permission for the Builders to hold anonymised extracts in their private working tools.
- A written reference when the mirror has been used on a live submission.

## 3. Gamma gets

- Free use of the mirror for as long as Gamma is a test partner: pre-check of its own applications and variations, the quote and schedule comparator, and the contract terms locator.
- The comparator and locator outputs in Gamma's own layouts where that is a label change, not new code.
- First refusal on any future paid module for subcontractors.

## 4. Ownership and confidentiality

- All code, designs, rules and documents belong to the Builders. Gamma's data belongs to Gamma.
- Gamma's rates, margins, client names and contracts are not disclosed; the Builders may describe the workbench and results without identifying Gamma unless Gamma agrees in writing.
- Neither side pays the other. Anything Gamma wants that has no consultant use is quoted separately.

## 5. Exit

- Either side may stop with 30 days' notice. Gamma keeps the outputs already produced; the Builders keep the code; Gamma's data is deleted from the Builders' tools on request.

- "Is there anything Gamma would pay for that is not on this list?" (record the answer; do not commit)
